SDS 261 Final Project

A project I completed for Professor Jo Hardin's interterm class, SDS 261. Using the R packages stringr and rvest (link to both of them), I scraped information from the Metropolitan Museum of Art's website to create a table containing key information on each piece created from the year 1800 to 1900 in their European Paintings collection. In the future, I believe it could be interesting to extend this web scraping to collect data on all the pieces in the Met's other collections, especially their contemporary exhibit. I would also like to do this with other museums' collections too, perhaps the MOMA and the Brooklyn Museum.
