EasyQualtrics

A package I created in collaboration with my classmate, Claire Kelly, for our final project for SDS 270, Advanced Programming in R. Our package, EasyQualtrics, aims to make importing csv files that have previously been exported from Qualtrics into R a smooth and seamless process.
