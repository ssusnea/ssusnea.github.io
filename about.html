About Sarah

Sarah Susnea (she/her) is currently completing a BA in Statistical and Data Sciences with a minor in Sociology at Smith College. She is also an intern at The United Way of Hampshire and Franklin Counties and a Statistics tutor and teaching assistant through Smith’s Spinelli Center for Quantitative Learning. She has a budding interest in urbanism as it blends together her studies in Data Science and Sociology and enjoys creating visualizations using traffic data. Ultimately Sarah is excited about all things data science and finds the problem solving required from quantitative analysis rewarding. She aims to use her skills in data science and quantitative analysis to assist in making deliberate and impactful decisions. In her free time, she enjoys kayaking, solving sudoku puzzles, and daydreaming about all the restaurants in NYC that she may one day eat at.
